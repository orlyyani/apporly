Debtly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Debtly is made by apporly ("I"). This policy explains how the Debtly app handles your information.

What Debtly stores

The debts, payments, notes and settings you enter are saved only in the app's private storage on your device.

What Debtly collects

Nothing. Debtly does not ask for an account, does not have internet permission, and does not send any data off your device. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Backups

If you choose "Back up my debts", Debtly creates a file with your debts and payments and opens Android's share menu. Where that file goes (Drive, email, your Files app) is up to you; Debtly doesn't upload it anywhere. "Restore from a backup" reads a file you pick and replaces the data on your device.

Sharing

Your data is never sold or shared, because it never leaves your device.

Deleting your data

Delete a debt inside the app, or uninstall Debtly (or clear its storage in Android Settings) to remove everything. I can't recover deleted data, since I never had a copy.

Children

Debtly is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
