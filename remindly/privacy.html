Remindly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Remindly is made by apporly ("I"). This policy explains how the Remindly app handles your information.

What Remindly stores

Your reminders, their history, your settings and the optional display name you type in are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

What Remindly collects

I don't collect anything. Remindly has no account system, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Optional Google Calendar sync

Sync is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings and sign in with Google, Remindly uses Google's Calendar API directly from your phone to create, update and delete events for your reminders in your own Google Calendar. This needs only the "view and edit events on your calendars" permission (calendar.events). The events contain the reminder's name, notes and dates. They go only to your Google account; I never receive or store them. Google's handling of that data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can turn sync off in Settings at any time, and remove Remindly's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Remindly's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Backups

If you choose "Back up", Remindly creates a file and opens Android's share menu. Where that file goes (Drive, email, your Files app) is up to you.

Sharing

I don't sell or share your data with anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete reminders inside the app, or uninstall Remindly (or clear its storage in Android Settings) to remove everything on your device. Events already synced to Google Calendar stay there until you delete them in Google Calendar.

Children

Remindly is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
